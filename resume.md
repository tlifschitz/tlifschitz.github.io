## Experience

### [Maper](https://home.mapertech.com) — Buenos Aires, Argentina

*Industrial IoT startup developing in-house hardware and software for predictive maintenance. Contributed to scaling the company's wireless vibration monitoring platform from prototype to over 10,000 deployed devices.*

#### Embedded Systems Technical Lead · Apr 2025 – Present

- **Own technical direction** across firmware, sensor hardware, and device-cloud integration for an industrial sensor platform, mentoring a small team while staying hands-on on critical-path firmware.
- **Diagnosed and resolved** a Thread network scaling failure at our largest deployment (~800 devices): partitioned the site across 4 channels and built auto channel-scan firmware; site now runs 1,000+ devices with stable connectivity.
- **Drive next-generation sensor platform** development across mechanical, electronics, and firmware: enclosure redesign of injection-molded and machined parts for water ingress and manufacturing yield, plus a space-constrained rigid-flex PCB and a lower-power accelerometer plus magnetic field sensor. In validation. Specified requirements and ran prototype iterations with external suppliers.
- **Build field-serviceability firmware**: NFC commissioning with a mobile diagnostics app and automatic TX power tuning — targeting shorter installs, fewer unnecessary replacements, and lower power draw.

#### Senior Embedded Systems Engineer · Jul 2023 – Mar 2025

- Coded the firmware of wireless vibration sensors, sustaining 10 rollouts per year with 100% sensor adoption. This firmware has supported the scaling of the company from 0 to 6,000 sensors in the field by early 2025.
- Implemented automatic builds and test pipelines across firmware products, reducing deployment time by 50% and making releases more reliable.
- Designed and implemented an advanced continuous vibration capture mechanism, unlocking new business opportunities in niche markets by monitoring specific asset types.

#### Embedded Systems Engineer · Mar 2021 – Jun 2023

- Performed full firmware lifecycle, from development to firmware release and OTA updates.
- Optimized vibration sensor low-power consumption, increasing battery life by 50%.
- Implemented DSP algorithms to improve the sensor monitoring features.
- Engineered an automatic testbench using a Raspberry Pi to perform end-of-line QA checks.

#### Embedded Systems Developer Intern · Aug 2019 – Feb 2021

- Defined requirements, designed hardware and firmware for a wireless vibration sensor.
- Performed PCB bring-up, and defined and executed validation tests using lab equipment.
- Developed drivers for onboard sensors and integrated the OpenThread protocol stack into application code.
