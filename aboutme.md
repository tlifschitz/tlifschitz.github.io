### Professional Background
- I am the Embedded Systems Technical Lead at [Maper](https://home.mapertech.com), a B2B industrial IoT startup building wireless vibration sensors for predictive maintenance. I've been part of scaling the platform from prototype to over 10,000 deployed devices, and today I own the technical direction across firmware, sensor hardware, and device-cloud integration.
- Some highlights of my work at Maper: diagnosing and resolving a Thread mesh scaling failure at a 1,000+ device site, designing a continuous vibration capture mechanism that opened new markets, and driving the development of the next-generation sensor platform across mechanical, electronics, and firmware.
- I am also a contributor to [SimpleBLE](https://github.com/OpenBluetoothToolbox/SimpleBLE), an open-source portable C++ BLE stack.
- As a contractor for a healthcare startup I reverse-engineered Bluetooth communication protocols of [some commercial glucose meters](https://github.com/sivulich/AppiaGlucometers). This project helped to integrate these devices into their application and unlocked a partnership between the contracting startup and J&J.

### Technical Stack

I have a broad range of technical skills, involving almost all the abstraction layers of a solution. This allows me to have a holistic view of the whole architecture of the system I am working on.

My expertise includes hardware design, firmware development, application software, and some backend knowledge. Additionally, I am familiar with common data analysis and machine learning libraries, and I am always eager to learn new things.

* **Embedded & Firmware**: C, C++, RTOS (ChibiOS), ARM Cortex-M4, Embedded Linux (Yocto), DMA, DSP (FFT, FIR, IIR, decimation)
* **Connectivity**: Thread / Matter, MQTT, CoAP, UDP, MODBUS, CAN, SPI, UART, I2C
* **Hardware & Lab**: PCB design (Altium Designer), LTSpice, JTAG/SWD, oscilloscope, power profiler, 3D printing
* **Data / ML**: Python, Jupyter, Grafana, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Scikit-Learn, TensorFlow
* **Backend**: Django, PostgreSQL, AWS EC2, Nginx
* **DevOps**: Git, Docker, CMake, Gitlab CI/CD, Github Actions, Mender
